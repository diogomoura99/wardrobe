What suits you, based on your photos

  • Cropped, boxy tops with wide trousers is your best shape. You're tall with long legs, so you carry wide trousers easily, and tops that end at the hip balance them. Your best photos show this (brown puffer, check jacket), and you've noticed it yourself. The pieces on this page lean cropped and boxy for that reason. Avoid tops that cover the zip of your trousers.
  • Rise matters with cropped tops. Go for mid or high-rise trousers so no skin shows when you lift your arms. Tuck long knits loosely at the front or fold them under at the hem.
  • Navy, brown and blue beat black on you. With dark hair and light skin, navy and dark brown give contrast without looking harsh. Your black puffer is the weakest piece in your photos, and the brown one does the same job better.
  • Blue is your colour. It's in five of your eight photos (linen shirt, stripes, suede sneakers, denim) and it always works.
  • Hem length: wide trousers should touch the shoe with a small fold, not swallow it. A few of your jeans pile up too much at the bottom. A tailor can take them up for about €10.
  • Accessories: silver jewellery gives an outfit character without being loud. Try a silver chain bracelet or cuff on the wrist opposite your watch, and a thin chain or small pendant at the neck. Most outfits here use one or two pieces.
  • Graphics: one fun graphic piece per outfit (Guinness knit, rugby polo, stripes) keeps it personal. Pair it with plain, darker trousers so it looks intentional.
